News: Principles for Responsible Investment marks 20th anniversary at the London Stock Exchange

27 April 2026 · Alfred Anate Mayaki

Original IBR source ↗

The Principles for Responsible Investment (PRI) marked the twentieth anniversary of its launch with the London Stock Exchange hosting the opening of a global series of events across major financial centres.

Launched in 2006 with the support of then United Nations Secretary-General Kofi Annan, PRI has grown into a global network of more than 5,000 signatories representing almost US$140 trillion in assets under management. Its purpose is to help investors incorporate environmental, social and governance considerations into investment practice and long-term risk management.

The anniversary comes as investors face increasingly interconnected geopolitical, technological and environmental pressures. These can affect long-term returns in ways that traditional financial analysis alone may not fully capture.

FTSE Russell, part of London Stock Exchange Group, was among PRI’s founding signatories in 2006. LSEG said the anniversary also coincides with 25 years since the launch of the FTSE4Good Index Series, reflecting the development of sustainable finance markets.

PRI Interim CEO Cambria Allen-Ratzlaff described responsible investment as a core part of how investors understand risk and return, while noting that current risks are more complex and interconnected.

The anniversary programme provides an opportunity to review progress and consider how responsible investment practices should evolve in response to changing market conditions.

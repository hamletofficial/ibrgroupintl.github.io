Press Release: FCA invites ESG rating providers to join voluntary reporting pilot

29 April 2026 · Financial Conduct Authority

Original IBR source ↗

The UK's Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) invited ESG rating providers to participate in a voluntary reporting pilot designed to improve transparency, consistency and trust in ESG assessments.

Participating firms would share structured information about their methodologies, data sources, governance and controls. The FCA said the pilot would help identify good practice and potential gaps, inform future policy thinking and potential guidance, and support a more coherent regulatory framework.

Providers of different sizes and business models were encouraged to express interest, with the FCA seeking a diverse range of methodologies and asset-class coverage.

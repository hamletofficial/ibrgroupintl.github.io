News: CBRE to support delivery of Greater Manchester's Transport Vision

15 July 2025 · Alfred Anate Mayaki

Original IBR source ↗

CBRE has been appointed to a four-year Transport for Greater Manchester (TfGM) Professional Services Framework to support the region's transport vision. The framework covers a range of consultancy services for TfGM and participating authorities and is intended to support delivery of the Greater Manchester Transport Strategy 2040.

CBRE's appointment is under Lot 15, Estates Advisory Services. Its work will include property-asset management and development, land assembly and strategic advice to enable new and improved transport facilities across Greater Manchester.

The appointment builds on CBRE's longstanding relationship with TfGM, including work acquiring bus depots to support Greater Manchester's bus reform programme. The wider regional transport agenda includes the Bee Network, expansion of the Metrolink tram system and investment in cycling and walking infrastructure.

CBRE also described work with TfGM on surplus land and the potential to unlock sites for affordable, net-zero homes, linking transport investment with regeneration and housing objectives.

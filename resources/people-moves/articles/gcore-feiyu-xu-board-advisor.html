People Moves: Gcore welcomes Prof. Dr. Feiyu Xu as Board Advisor

28 May 2025 · IBR media release summary

Prof. Dr. Feiyu Xu · Board Advisor · Gcore

Original IBR source / press release ↗

Gcore appointed Prof. Dr. Feiyu Xu as a Board Advisor, adding AI research and industry expertise to its strategic leadership. The appointment was presented as supporting the company's innovation agenda and its development of edge AI infrastructure.

Gcore provides edge, cloud, network and security infrastructure across six continents. The company said Xu would advise on strategic AI initiatives and help guide the development of its AI capabilities.

Xu's appointment reflects the growing strategic importance of AI expertise for infrastructure providers building and deploying AI workloads across distributed cloud and edge environments.

Official source: Gcore. The original IBR article links to a PR Newswire announcement.

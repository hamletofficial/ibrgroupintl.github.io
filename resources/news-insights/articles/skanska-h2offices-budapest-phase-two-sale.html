News: Skanska sells second phase of H2Offices in Budapest for EUR 98 million

28 September 2026 · IBR article summary

Skanska; ERSTE Open-Ended Real Estate Investment Fund

Original IBR source / press release ↗

Skanska announced the divestment of the second phase of the H2Offices office complex in Budapest, Hungary, for EUR 98 million (approximately SEK 1.1 billion). The buyer is ERSTE Open-Ended Real Estate Investment Fund, represented by ERSTE Alapkezelő Zrt.

The transaction was to be recorded by Skanska Commercial Development Europe in the third quarter of 2026, with transfer scheduled for the fourth quarter of 2027. The second phase will provide approximately 22,000 square metres of leasable area and was reported as fully leased to a global company under a 10-year lease. Completion was scheduled for the first quarter of 2027.

People Moves: Newmark appoints Mike Whitaker as Group Chief Information Officer

28 September 2026 · IBR media release summary

Mike Whitaker · Group Chief Information Officer · Newmark Group, Inc.

Original IBR source / press release ↗

Newmark Group, Inc. announced the appointment of Mike Whitaker as Group Chief Information Officer. He will report to Chief Operating Officer Luis Alvarado and work with Chief Strategy Officer Kyle Lutnick on the company's technology, data and information strategy. Whitaker will also join Newmark's Executive Committee.

In the role, Whitaker will oversee technology, data and information initiatives across Newmark's businesses. The company's existing Chief Information Officer, Sridhar Potineni, and the technology leadership team will report to him.

News: Colliers partners with global infrastructure investment bank

5 June 2025 · Alfred Anate Mayaki

Original IBR source ↗

Colliers announced a definitive agreement to acquire a controlling interest in Astris Infrastructure, LLC, known as Astris Finance, a global investment-banking firm specialising in infrastructure and the energy transition. The transaction was expected to close in the third quarter of 2025, subject to customary conditions; financial terms were not disclosed.

Under the partnership model, Astris Finance's senior leadership and nine team members who would become partners were expected to retain significant ownership. The firm had 65 professionals across nine offices in the Americas, Europe and Asia.

Astris Finance advises on mergers and acquisitions, project finance and long-term capital raising for developers and investors in transportation, renewable energy, power generation and distribution, and digital and social infrastructure.

Colliers said the acquisition would expand its investment-banking capabilities and support clients investing in infrastructure related to urbanisation, energy security and decarbonisation.

People Moves: Spencer Stuart appoints Justin Zale to lead its Global Real Estate practice

16 April 2026 · By Alfred Anate Mayaki

Justin Zale · Leader, Global Real Estate Practice · Spencer Stuart

Original IBR source / press release ↗

New York (PR Newswire) -- Spencer Stuart announced that Justin Zale has been appointed to lead its Global Real Estate Practice. In this role, he will partner with investors, developers and occupiers around the world to build high-performing leadership teams and boards.

Zale brings experience across commercial, residential and alternative real estate. Before joining Spencer Stuart, he was a partner at another global executive search and leadership advisory firm, where he helped develop and lead its global real estate practice. Earlier, he worked as a real estate industry practitioner focused on investment and capital advisory, co-founded S2K Partners, and held merchant banking and investment banking roles at Cantor Fitzgerald.

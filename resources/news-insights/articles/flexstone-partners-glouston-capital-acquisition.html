Press Release: Flexstone Partners to acquire Glouston Capital Partners to form $15 billion private equity platform

18 June 2026 · Flexstone Partners; Glouston Capital Partners

Original IBR source / press release ↗

New York and Boston (Business Wire) -- Flexstone Partners, a global private-markets investment manager and affiliate of Natixis Investment Managers, announced an agreement to acquire Glouston Capital Partners, a Boston-based private-equity investment manager.

The combination was described as creating a private-equity platform with approximately $15 billion in assets under management. The announcement outlined a strategic combination of the firms' investment capabilities and client relationships. Readers should consult the original announcement for the full terms, conditions and transaction details.

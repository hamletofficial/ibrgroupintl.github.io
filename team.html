Alfred Anate Bodurin Mayaki

Managing Director & Head of Energy and Infrastructure Derivatives Sector Recruitment

Biography

Alfred Anate Bodurin Mayaki is a labour economist, photojournalist and recruitment executive whose work combines executive search, market intelligence, leadership advisory and editorial research.

As Managing Director and Head of Energy and Infrastructure Derivatives Sector Recruitment at Impact in Business Recruitment, he focuses on senior recruitment and sector-led search, with particular interest in energy, infrastructure and financial markets. IBR describes its specialist practice as centred on energy and infrastructure, alongside executive search, human resources and people analytics.

His professional and academic interests span labour economics, game theory, cooperative signalling and incomplete-contracts theory. His published and working research has examined strategic interaction, wage dynamics, labour-market institutions and policy questions, including the relationship between cooperation, competition and stable outcomes.

He also serves as Lead Editor of the Impact in Business Recruitment Blog, contributing commentary and research-led analysis on leadership, sustainability, financial markets, labour and wider economic affairs.

His approach to recruitment is grounded in analytical rigour, sector knowledge and an emphasis on matching leadership capability with the institutional and commercial requirements of the organisation.

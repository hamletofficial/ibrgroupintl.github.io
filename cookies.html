Cookies Policy

Effective date: April 2026

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device to store information about your visit. They help websites remember preferences and provide a better browsing experience.

How we use cookies

We use cookies for essential site functionality (session management), analytics (to improve the Site) and optional features such as remembering preference settings. Third-party services (for example Google Analytics and Stripe) may set cookies when you use those features.

Types of cookies we use

  • Essential cookies — required for core functionality (sign-in, security).
  • Performance & analytics — used to measure and improve how the Site is used.
  • Functional — remember user preferences and settings.
  • Third-party — cookies set by external providers for payments, analytics or widgets.

Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. If you disable cookies, some parts of the Site may not function correctly. To manage consent on this Site use the cookie bar presented in the footer.

Changes

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time. We will post changes on this page with an updated effective date.

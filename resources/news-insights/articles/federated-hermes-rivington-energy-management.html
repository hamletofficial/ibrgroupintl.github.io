News: Federated Hermes Limited acquires majority stake in Rivington Energy Management

8 April 2025 · Alfred Anate Mayaki

Original IBR source ↗

Federated Hermes Limited completed the acquisition of a majority stake in Rivington Energy Management Limited, a UK-based renewable-energy project development business. The transaction combines Rivington's specialist project development expertise with Federated Hermes' institutional investment and asset-management capabilities.

Rivington operates across energy development, innovation and project management. Its Renewable Connections division originates and secures consent for renewable-energy projects and had consented more than 1 GW of solar and storage across 25 UK projects since 2021, according to the announcement.

The business's areas of expertise include energy-efficient data centres, solar repowering, business-to-business energy solutions, and co-located solar and battery energy storage. The acquisition was intended to add proprietary opportunities to Federated Hermes' energy-transition investment pipeline.

Rivington's London-based team comprised approximately 40 people. The business joined Federated Hermes' existing UK and European infrastructure platform.

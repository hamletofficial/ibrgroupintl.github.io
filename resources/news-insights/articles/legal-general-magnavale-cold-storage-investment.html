News: L&G expands private credit portfolio with £250m Magnavale Cold Storage investment

13 June 2025 · Alfred Anate Mayaki

Original IBR source ↗

L&G announced the completion of a £250 million private debt investment with Magnavale Ltd over a ten-year period. The overall debt facility totals £500 million and was co-arranged by L&G and a US institutional investor. The transaction expands L&G's real estate debt portfolio into cold storage and logistics infrastructure.

Magnavale operates four temperature-controlled storage facilities serving the food industry. Its cold-chain services help manage seasonal demand and supply disruptions, and can contribute to reducing food waste and supporting food security.

The company has invested in energy-efficient facilities and expanded network capacity. In February 2025, it opened a cold store at its Easton site with 101,000 automated pallet spaces; across all sites, its combined capacity exceeds 350,000 pallet positions.

L&G said the investment supports critical supply-chain infrastructure and long-term growth in the UK cold-storage sector. The lender group was advised by Clifford Chance and CBRE, while Macfarlanes advised Magnavale.

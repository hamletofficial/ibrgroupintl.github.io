People Moves: OMERS appoints Luca Lupo as Infrastructure leader for Europe

23 March 2026 · By Alfred Anate Mayaki

Luca Lupo · Senior Managing Director and Head of Europe · OMERS Infrastructure

Ontario, Canada (Press Release) -- OMERS Infrastructure announced the appointment of Luca Lupo as Senior Managing Director and Head of Europe. He reports to Michael Hill, Executive Vice President and Global Head of OMERS Infrastructure, and joins the Infrastructure Management Committee.

Since joining OMERS in 2022, Lupo has contributed to the infrastructure leadership team, including originating Grandi Stazioni Retail (GSR), marking OMERS' entry into Italy, and supporting capital rotation and asset-management initiatives. He serves on the boards of GSR and Associated British Ports.

In the role, Lupo leads the regional team based in London and oversees regional origination and management of the platform's European investments. The original article lists James Thompson, Director of Investment Communications at OMERS, as the media contact.

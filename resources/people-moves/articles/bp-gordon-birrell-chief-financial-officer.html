People Moves: BP appoints Gordon Birrell as Chief Financial Officer

22 May 2025 · IBR media release summary

Gordon Birrell · Chief Financial Officer · BP plc

Original IBR source ↗

BP announced Gordon Birrell's appointment as Chief Financial Officer, effective 1 July 2025. Birrell had led BP's production and operations business, with responsibility for upstream operations and projects, including major production, gas and low-carbon activities.

He joined BP in 1997 and held senior leadership positions across the company's upstream, downstream and integrated gas businesses. The appointment was part of BP's executive leadership succession planning and aimed to bring operational experience to the finance function.

The appointment was announced as a planned transition and should be read in the context of BP's subsequent leadership updates.

Company source: BP.

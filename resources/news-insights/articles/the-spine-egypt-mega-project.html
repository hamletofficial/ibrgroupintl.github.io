News: The Spine — Inside Egypt’s new mega project

27 April 2026 · Alfred Anate Mayaki

Original IBR source ↗

The Spine is a proposed $27 billion (EGP 1.4 trillion) urban development launched in April 2026 by Talaat Moustafa Group in partnership with the National Bank of Egypt. Located east of Cairo within the existing Madinaty development, the project has been presented as a “cognitive city” integrating artificial intelligence and Internet of Things technologies into a car-free urban environment.

The scheme is planned across 2.4 million square metres of land, with approximately 3.8 million square metres of built-up area. Plans described in the source include around 20,000 residential units, 1,251 serviced apartments, 580,000 square metres of Grade A+ office space and 565,000 square metres for retail, dining and entertainment.

The project also envisages 3,500 hotel rooms and suites, 165 towers reaching up to 130 metres, and more than one million square metres of landscaped green areas and water features.

Its location is positioned to connect established residential and economic hubs in Greater Cairo with the New Administrative Capital and regional transport corridors. The project's stated scale places it among the region's major planned mixed-use urban developments.

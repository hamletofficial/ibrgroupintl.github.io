News: Acuity advises One Big Circle on its strategic partnership with Loram

16 April 2026 · By Alfred Anate Mayaki

Acuity · One Big Circle · Loram

Bristol, England (Press Release) -- Acuity announced that it acted as exclusive strategic adviser to One Big Circle on its new partnership with Loram. The collaboration brings together One Big Circle's rail technology and Loram's track maintenance expertise to accelerate digital transformation across the rail sector. By combining complementary capabilities, the partnership aims to deliver safer, more efficient, and data-driven infrastructure solutions for rail operators worldwide.

One Big Circle, based in Bristol, is a provider of AI-powered rail infrastructure monitoring and digital inspection technology. The company is best known for its Automated Intelligent Video Review (AIVR) platform, a computer vision and data analytics solution that enables rail operators and infrastructure owners to capture, analyse and manage large volumes of lineside video and asset data. By applying machine learning, advanced video analytics and cloud-based data management, AIVR helps infrastructure managers improve inspection efficiency, enable predictive maintenance, and enhance the safety and reliability of critical rail networks.

Loram is a global provider of rail maintenance and inspection services. Through this partnership, Loram will combine its operational expertise and international reach with One Big Circle's AI-enabled infrastructure monitoring technology, supporting the deployment of AIVR across global rail networks and accelerating the digital transformation of the railway.

People Moves: Macquarie Capital names Sean Yajnik Head of Digital Infrastructure Advisory – Americas

4 June 2025 · IBR media release summary

Sean Yajnik · Head of Digital Infrastructure Advisory – Americas · Macquarie Capital

Original IBR source / press release ↗

Macquarie Capital, the advisory, capital markets and principal investment arm of Macquarie Group, appointed Sean Yajnik as Head of Digital Infrastructure Advisory – Americas. He will lead the firm's advisory business in the Americas, partnering with corporate and financial sponsor clients on mergers and acquisitions, capital raising and debt advisory.

Yajnik brings nearly two decades of advisory experience across data centres, consumer broadband, enterprise fibre and wireless infrastructure. He joined from TD Securities, where he specialised in digital infrastructure client coverage and M&A in the United States, and previously held senior roles at Société Générale and TAP Advisors.

Macquarie Capital operates across digital, energy, transport and social infrastructure and described the appointment as supporting continued growth in its digital infrastructure advisory practice.

Official source: Macquarie Group.

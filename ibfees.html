IBR Fees and Listings Access Policy

Effective Date: April 2026

Last Updated: 18 October 2026

1. Commission on residential and commercial listings

Impact in Business Recruitment Group Limited (“IBR”) does not charge commission in relation to residential or commercial property listings. No listing commission, transaction success fee or percentage of a sale, purchase, lease or letting is payable to IBR merely because a property is submitted, published, viewed, introduced through or discussed using Hamlet.

A software licence fee does apply for use of the Hamlet platform. This is a charge for software/platform access, not a property commission. Applicable subscription options and prices are set out on the Pricing page.

2. Information you choose to publish

Brokers should only submit details that they are content to have made publicly available through the listings gallery and associated member-facing property resources.

This includes contact information such as email addresses and/or telephone numbers. Do not submit confidential, commercially sensitive, personal, or third-party information unless you have the right and appropriate authority to disclose it.

3. Responsibility for listing information

Users are liable for the information they post in the listings gallery. You should ensure that property descriptions, availability information, contact details, URLs and other particulars are accurate, lawful and suitable for publication.

By submitting a listing, you accept responsibility for the content associated with that listing and for ensuring that you have the necessary authority to provide it.

4. Fraud, misuse and authentication

Fraudulent, misleading, abusive or otherwise improper use of the property listings service will be dealt with swiftly in accordance with our applicable policy on user authentication and account security.

Access to the listings service is tied to authenticated user accounts. We may suspend or remove access where information appears fraudulent, materially misleading, unauthorised, or otherwise inconsistent with our site rules and security requirements.

5. Practical guidance for brokers

  • Only publish information you wish to be made publicly available.
  • Check property and contact details before submitting a listing.
  • Do not use the listings gallery to publish confidential information.
  • Report suspected fraud or materially misleading information promptly to support@mail.hamlet-ai.co.uk.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy, the introductory commission arrangement or a listing should be directed to support@mail.hamlet-ai.co.uk.

7. Scope of service and no agency appointment

Unless separately agreed in a written instruction signed by the relevant parties, access to the platform is a listing and broker-network facility only. It does not, by itself, appoint Impact in Business Recruitment Group Limited (“IBR”) as an estate agent, introduce a client on exclusive terms, or authorise IBR to negotiate, agree, or bind any party to a sale, letting, lease, or other property transaction.

Any agency appointment must be documented separately, with its scope, duration, authority and remuneration clearly agreed in writing.

8. Software licence fees and other charges

The software licence or subscription fee is charged for access to the Hamlet platform in accordance with the applicable plan and pricing published on the Pricing page. The fee is separate from property listings and is not a commission on a residential or commercial property, a sale, a purchase, a lease or a letting. Premium membership is an annual plan; other available subscription options are described on the Pricing page.

IBR does not charge commission in relation to residential or commercial listings. No separate transaction fee, success fee, referral payment, marketing expense or other charge should be inferred from a listing or from this policy. If IBR offers a genuinely separate paid service or a third-party cost may arise, its scope, amount or calculation, payment trigger and any applicable VAT must be disclosed in writing and agreed before the service begins.

9. Introductions and non-circumvention

Use of Hamlet, or an introduction arising from a residential or commercial listing, does not create a commission entitlement, success fee or non-circumvention obligation in favour of IBR. IBR does not charge commission in relation to residential or commercial listings.

Any genuinely separate arrangement between other parties is independent of IBR's software licence fee and must be documented and agreed by those parties themselves. Members must not represent that IBR is entitled to a transaction fee or commission unless a separate, lawful written agreement expressly establishes that entitlement.

10. Conflicts of interest and referral benefits

Members must disclose any material conflict of interest relevant to a proposed property transaction and must not misrepresent whom they represent. Where IBR or a member may receive a referral fee, commission, gift or other benefit connected with a recommendation or introduction, the relevant interest should be disclosed clearly and in writing to the affected party before they rely on that recommendation.

11. Due diligence and property particulars

Members should undertake proportionate checks before marketing or relying on property information, including their authority to act, the identity and authority of the instructing party, material title or occupational information available to them, and any restrictions on marketing or disclosure. Do not describe a property as available, verified, authorised or exclusively instructed unless you have reasonable grounds to do so.

Platform publication is not a survey, valuation, title investigation, legal review, planning assessment, or verification of a property's ownership, condition, income, or suitability. Interested parties should obtain their own legal, tax, valuation, building and other professional advice as appropriate.

12. Anti-fraud, sanctions and source of funds

Members must not use the service to facilitate fraud, bribery, money laundering, terrorist financing, sanctions evasion, or any unlawful transaction. Where applicable to their role, members remain responsible for complying with their own legal and regulatory obligations, including identity checks, customer due diligence, record keeping and suspicious-activity reporting.

IBR may request reasonable supporting information, restrict a listing, or suspend access while a credible concern is reviewed. Any action will be subject to applicable law and the relevant contractual terms.

13. Confidentiality, privacy and data protection

Do not upload confidential information or personal data unless you have a lawful basis and authority to share it for the stated purpose. Share only the information reasonably necessary, keep it accurate, and take appropriate steps to protect it from unauthorised access or disclosure.

Personal data handled through the platform is also subject to the applicable Privacy Policy and data protection law. Members remain responsible for their own handling of information they obtain from other members and prospective counterparties.

14. Intellectual property and permitted use

Members must have permission to use photographs, floor plans, descriptions, logos, documents and other material they submit. By uploading material, a member confirms that doing so does not knowingly infringe another person's rights and grants IBR the limited permission reasonably required to host, display and operate the listing service.

Members must not scrape, bulk-copy, resell, republish or otherwise misuse listing data or platform content, except where authorised by IBR or permitted by law.

15. Listing moderation, suspension and removal

IBR may review, decline, amend for presentation, hide or remove a listing where it reasonably considers that the listing is inaccurate, unauthorised, unlawful, misleading, unsafe, reported as fraudulent, or inconsistent with the platform's terms or this policy. Where practicable, IBR may seek clarification before taking action, but urgent protective action may be taken where appropriate.

Members should promptly update or withdraw listings that are no longer available or whose material particulars have changed.

16. No guarantee and limitation of responsibility

The platform is provided to help members share and discover information. IBR does not guarantee that a listing is accurate, that a transaction will proceed, that a counterparty is suitable, or that a property will meet a user's requirements. Members must make their own enquiries and decisions.

Nothing in this policy excludes or limits liability where doing so would be unlawful, including liability that cannot legally be excluded. Any other allocation of liability must be read subject to the applicable terms of service and any separately signed agreement.

17. Complaints and dispute handling

Raise policy, listing or fee concerns promptly and in writing with support@mail.hamlet-ai.co.uk, providing the listing reference, relevant dates and supporting details. IBR will review the information available and may request further evidence from the parties involved.

Members should seek to resolve commercial disagreements professionally and preserve relevant records. This process does not prevent a party from exercising any legal rights or remedies available to them.

18. Changes to this policy

IBR may update this policy to reflect changes to the service, commercial practice or applicable law. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated revision date. Changes to any separately agreed fee or agency appointment will be governed by the terms of that agreement and applicable law; publication of a revised policy does not, by itself, vary a separate contract.

19. Applicable law and interpretation

This policy should be read alongside the site's Terms of Service, Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy. It does not replace a signed agency agreement, property contract or professional advice. Applicable law and jurisdiction are determined by the relevant contractual terms and mandatory legal rules; nothing in this section is intended to override those rules.

References to legal or professional standards are intended as practical guidance and do not imply that IBR is regulated by, or is a member of, a professional body unless that status is separately and accurately stated.

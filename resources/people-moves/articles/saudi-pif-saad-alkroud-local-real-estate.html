People Moves: Saudi PIF appoints Saad Alkroud to lead local real estate

22 May 2025 · Alfred Anate Mayaki

Original IBR source ↗

Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund (PIF) appointed Saad Alkroud as Head of the Local Real Estate Investment Division. Alkroud had served as PIF's Chief of Staff since 2021, having joined the fund in 2016 in stakeholder management.

In the new role, he is responsible for developing PIF's local real estate and infrastructure investment portfolio, including its portfolio of strategic developments and major projects. His previous responsibilities included board and committee affairs, international relations, corporate strategy, business planning and portfolio-company matters.

The announcement followed other senior leadership changes at PIF and its portfolio, as the fund continued to build out its domestic investment activities.

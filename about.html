About Impact in Business Recruitment

What does IB provide? How does it work?

IB is a full-service, agile, remote recruitment process outsourcing firm. We help employers source and collect resumes efficiently, giving hiring managers more time to focus on operational responsibilities.

What IB provides

An award-winning recruitment and communications service

IB supports the recruiting process from understanding the role to presenting a shortlist, while the hiring manager remains in control of the final decision.

Full-service and agile RPO

A remote recruitment process outsourcing partner that can adapt its support to your job order requirements and hiring priorities.

Cost-efficient sourcing support

An alternative to handling resume sourcing and collection entirely in-house, helping make the hiring process more efficient.

More time for operations

Recruiter-led sourcing and candidate collection lets hiring managers dedicate more time to their operational duties.

How it works

Three clear steps, from request to shortlist

A quick, seamless request is followed by recruiter review and a focused conversation about your requirements.

1

Submit your hiring request

Share the role and hiring context through the request form. The recruiter reviews your submission and prepares to discuss the requirements.

2

Discuss requirements by phone

Have one phone call with the recruiter to clarify the role, priorities, and candidate profile before sourcing begins.

3

Review a shortlist

The recruiter sources and collects resumes, reviews candidates against the agreed requirements, and shares a shortlist for your consideration.

✓

The final hiring decision is yours

IB supports the recruitment process and presents shortlisted candidates. The hiring manager makes the final decision about whom to hire.

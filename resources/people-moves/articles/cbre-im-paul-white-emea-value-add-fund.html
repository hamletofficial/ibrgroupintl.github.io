People Moves: CBRE Investment Management appoints Paul White to lead EMEA flagship Value-Add fund series

16 April 2026 · IBR media release summary

Paul White · Head of EMEA flagship Value-Add fund series · CBRE Investment Management

Original IBR source / press release ↗

CBRE Investment Management announced the appointment of Paul White as Head of its EMEA flagship Value-Add fund series. He will define and execute the fund's strategy, source and oversee transactions, and drive performance across key European markets.

White will work with the pan-European platform to identify opportunities in logistics, living and next-generation offices, using active asset management, repositioning and sustainability-led enhancements to seek long-term value for investors.

The source describes CBRE Investment Management as a global real assets investment manager operating across 20 countries.

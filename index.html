The hamlet difference

A clearer route to sustainable commercial space.

Hamlet brings commercial leasing workflows together around a clear market focus: sustainable properties with current, valid energy-efficiency credentials and Passivhaus or EnerPHit certification. Members can discover opportunities, organise leasing pipelines, manage resources and coordinate follow-up in one workspace. Certification and energy-performance evidence should be checked against the property documentation and relevant scheme records.

  • ✓ Sustainable commercial leasing opportunities
  • ✓ Valid energy-efficiency standards and evidence
  • ✓ Passivhaus or EnerPHit certification focus
  • ✓ Direct messaging and social updates
  • ✓ Authenticated member workspace
  • ✓ Clear routes to policies and support

Important: hamlet is a workflow and networking environment, not a guarantee that a listing is accurate, a transaction will complete, or a counterparty has been independently vetted. Carry out your own commercial, legal, financial and property due diligence.

On the horizon

The next chapter of hamlet is taking shape.

We’re preparing two AI-assisted tools designed to reduce fragmentation in commercial property workflows. The aim is practical support for brokers: keep deal progress clearer and make listing preparation more consistent, while leaving decisions and final approval with the professional.

In preparation

1. Deal Assistant

A focused AI companion for commercial property deal progression—helping users organise deal notes, summarise the information they provide, identify gaps and consider sensible next actions.

  • Structure and summarise user-provided deal notes.
  • Highlight missing information and potential critical-path risks.
  • Help turn context into a clearer list of follow-up actions.
In preparation

2. Listings Copilot

A drafting assistant for commercial property listings, designed to help brokers turn supplied property facts into clear, professional copy without filling gaps with unsupported claims.

  • Draft listing descriptions from facts supplied by the user.
  • Flag missing details for the broker to confirm.
  • Keep material claims—including areas, rents, energy credentials and certification—subject to human verification.

Built for professional oversight: these assistants are intended to support drafting and organisation, not replace broker judgement or property, legal, financial or environmental due diligence. AI-generated content must be reviewed before use. The tools are being prepared for release; availability and timing may change, and no launch date is being announced here.

Insights: Asia Pacific corporate real estate leaders accelerate innovation as AI reshapes portfolios

20 April 2026 · Alfred Anate Mayaki

Original IBR source ↗

Corporate real estate (CRE) leaders across Asia Pacific are increasingly embracing innovation, with artificial intelligence and workplace strategy emerging as central to portfolio decision-making, according to Colliers and CoreNet Global.

A global poll conducted at the 2025–2026 CoreNet Global Summit across Asia Pacific, Europe and North America surveyed more than 1,000 corporate real estate professionals. It found that AI was the leading global driver of transformation, cited by 51% of respondents.

Respondents in Asia Pacific reported strong intent to innovate across portfolio strategy, workplace experience and data-led decision-making. Singapore and India were identified among markets where talent, investment and experimentation are converging, alongside the UK and the San Francisco Bay Area.

Innovation becomes business-critical

The findings suggest that technology, particularly AI and automation, is increasingly viewed as a business essential rather than a future advantage. Occupiers are scaling AI-enabled tools for forecasting, utilisation analysis, site selection and performance measurement, shifting attention toward embedding innovation into routine decisions.

Mike Davis, Managing Director of Occupier Services for Asia Pacific at Colliers, said advanced analytics are shaping how occupiers evaluate portfolio performance, location strategy and employee experience. The article notes that organisations are seeking to align technology, people and strategy to support faster, better-informed decisions.

Regional influence

The research also highlights Asia Pacific's role in shaping global CRE practice, particularly in fast-growing markets where scale, talent and technology adoption intersect. The stated opportunity is to connect data and strategy, and to make innovation repeatable, scalable and aligned with broader business goals.

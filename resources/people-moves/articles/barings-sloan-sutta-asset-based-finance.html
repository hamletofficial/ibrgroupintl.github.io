People Moves: Barings appoints Sloan Sutta as Global Head of Asset-Based Finance

6 October 2026 · IBR media release summary

Sloan Sutta · Global Head of Asset-Based Finance · Barings

Original IBR source / press release ↗

Barings announced that Sloan Sutta has joined the firm as Global Head of Asset-Based Finance (ABF). He is based in New York and reports to Martin Horne, Co-Head of Global Investments.

Sutta will lead Barings' global ABF platform, reported by the source at approximately $60 billion, across public and private commercial, consumer and residential strategies. His remit includes the platform's strategic direction, investment capabilities and continued growth.

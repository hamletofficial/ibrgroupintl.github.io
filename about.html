Alfred Mayaki founded Hamlet to support a more connected way of working with commercial property opportunities and the deals that develop around them.

Hamlet is developed and operated in association with Impact in Business Recruitment Group Limited.

The idea behind Hamlet

One connected workspace for property and progress.

Commercial property transactions involve more than a listing. They require people to share information, keep track of discussions, coordinate due diligence and move through milestones before a deal can complete.

Hamlet brings property discovery and deal-pipeline management closer together, with the aim of reducing fragmentation and making the path from initial interest to execution easier to follow.

The product direction is practical: make relevant opportunities easier to find, keep deal information organised, and help participants see what needs attention next.

What Hamlet brings together

Designed around the commercial property journey

Hamlet connects the core activities involved in finding, evaluating and progressing sustainable commercial property opportunities.

⌂

Property listings

A dedicated place to discover and share commercial property opportunities, with a particular focus on sustainable buildings and relevant certification.

↗

Deal pipeline

Organise opportunities as they progress through stages such as LOI issued, due diligence, under contract and closed.

◎

Milestones and reminders

Keep important critical-path dates, review periods and contractual milestones visible so that key actions are less likely to be overlooked.

⇄

Professional collaboration

Support a broker community in which property opportunities and deal information can be connected to the people working on them.

▤

Shared deal context

Keep the progression of a transaction easier to understand, from initial interest and due diligence through contract and completion.

♧

Sustainability in focus

Prioritise commercial properties with valid energy-efficiency standards and Passivhaus or EnerPHit certification, in line with Hamlet's stated market focus.

Features and availability can evolve as Hamlet develops. Property certification and other listing details should be checked against the relevant supporting documentation before a transaction proceeds.

From opportunity to outcome

A clearer view of deal progression

Hamlet's pipeline is intended to make the steps in a transaction easier to follow rather than leaving progress scattered across disconnected tools.

Discover

Identify a commercial property opportunity and the people connected to it.

Evaluate

Organise due diligence, reviews and the information needed to assess the opportunity.

Progress

Track negotiations, contractual milestones and the next actions required.

Complete

Keep the route toward lease execution or purchase completion visible to the team.

Founder and stewardship

Built with a connected-deals mindset.

As founder, Alfred Mayaki sets the direction for Hamlet around a straightforward proposition: commercial property professionals should be able to connect listings with the work required to progress a deal.

Hamlet is associated with Impact in Business Recruitment Group Limited, which operates the platform. The product is being developed as a focused workspace for sustainable commercial property activity, rather than as a general-purpose property directory alone.

This page describes the product's purpose and stated direction; it does not imply that every planned capability is available to every user or that a particular transaction outcome is guaranteed.

Insights: What would planning policy look like with Andy Burnham as Prime Minister?

30 May 2026 · Simon Donohue

Original IBR source ↗

Simon Donohue's analysis considers how Andy Burnham's record as Mayor of Greater Manchester could inform a national approach to planning and housing if he were to become Prime Minister. The piece focuses on devolution, local control, brownfield-first development and a stronger role for public institutions working with private-sector partners.

It highlights the Greater Manchester Trailblazer devolution deal, including flexible funding and additional powers over housing, regeneration and transport, and notes the region's brownfield funding. The article also discusses social-rent housing ambitions, rental standards, the Places for Everyone plan, and the political tensions surrounding green-belt release and local development plans.

The analysis concludes that a Burnham-led approach might favour town-centre renewal, transport integration and affordable housing delivery, while facing the familiar challenge of balancing development with local opposition.

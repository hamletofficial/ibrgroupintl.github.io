People Moves: West Sussex Economic Growth Board appoints Henry Powell as independent Chair

25 September 2026 · IBR media release summary

Henry Powell · Independent Chair · West Sussex Economic Growth Board

Original IBR source / press release ↗

The West Sussex Economic Growth Board (WSEGB) appointed Henry Powell as its new independent Chair, marking a step in strengthening the county's strategic approach to economic development.

Powell will work with local authorities, business leaders and partners to champion sustainable growth, support innovation and create opportunities for residents and employers. The source describes his experience in business leadership and partnership working.

"Henry Powell's appointment as independent Chair of the West Sussex Economic Growth Board is fantastic news for West Sussex. His extensive business experience, proven leadership and commitment to supporting economic growth make him ideally placed to guide the Board's work."

Councillor Yvonne Gravely, Cabinet Member for Economy and Skills, West Sussex County Council

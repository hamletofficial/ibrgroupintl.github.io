News: Arthur Cox advises AIB on €80 million green loan to Lidl Ireland

11 April 2026 · Alfred Anate Mayaki

Original IBR source ↗

IRELAND (Press Release) — Arthur Cox has advised Allied Irish Banks, p.l.c. (AIB) on the provision of an €80 million green loan facility to Lidl Ireland. The financing will support Lidl’s continued investment in energy-efficient stores, low-carbon logistics, and sustainable infrastructure across its Irish operations. The transaction aligns with AIB’s strategy to grow its green lending portfolio and underlines Lidl’s commitment to ambitious environmental targets.

The firm's multidisciplinary team advised on all aspects of the facility, including structuring in line with market-standard green loan principles, documentation, and regulatory considerations. The deal further demonstrates Arthur Cox’s experience in complex sustainable finance transactions, working with leading domestic and international institutions to deliver robust, future-focused funding solutions.

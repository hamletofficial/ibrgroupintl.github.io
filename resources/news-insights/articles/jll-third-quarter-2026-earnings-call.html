News: JLL announces third-quarter 2026 earnings release and conference call

28 September 2026

Jones Lang LaSalle Incorporated

Chicago, Illinois (PR Newswire) -- Jones Lang LaSalle Incorporated (NYSE: JLL) announced a conference call and webcast to discuss third-quarter 2026 results on Wednesday, 28 October 2026, at 9:00 a.m. Eastern time.

The announcement provided a telephone access number and conference ID, and said the webcast would be available through JLL's Investor Relations website. Presentation slides were expected in the Events & Presentations section shortly before the webcast. A replay was to remain available for 12 months. The source listed JLL Investor Relations for further information.

About JLL

JLL is a global commercial real estate services and investment management company. The original release described operations in more than 80 countries and services spanning buying, building, occupying, managing and investing in property, alongside investment management through LaSalle Investment Management.

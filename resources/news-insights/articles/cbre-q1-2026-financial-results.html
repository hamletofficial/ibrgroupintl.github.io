News: CBRE reports first-quarter 2026 financial results

23 April 2026 · Alfred Anate Mayaki

Original IBR source ↗

CBRE Group, Inc. reported first-quarter 2026 results for the period ended 31 March 2026. The company reported GAAP earnings per share of $1.07, up 98%, and core earnings per share of $1.61, up 81%. Revenue increased 19% to $10.5 billion.

CBRE said revenue grew across both its Resilient and Transactional Businesses. Transactional Businesses revenue rose 22%, while revenue in the Resilient Businesses increased 18%. Cash flow from operations was nearly $1.3 billion and free cash flow nearly $1.7 billion on a trailing twelve-month basis.

The company raised its 2026 core EPS outlook to a range of $7.60–$7.80, from its previous range of $7.30–$7.60. Chief Executive Officer Bob Sulentic highlighted growth across Advisory, Building Operations & Experience and Project Management, and identified data-centre land development and infrastructure-related services as significant contributors.

Infrastructure-related work cited by CBRE includes services for data centres, power, telecommunications and transportation assets across its business segments.

Official investor information: CBRE Investor Relations. This page summarises the third-party financial announcement and links to the original IBR publication.

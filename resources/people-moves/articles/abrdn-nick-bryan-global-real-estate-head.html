People Moves: abrdn appoints Nick Bryan as Global Head of Real Estate

19 May 2025 · IBR media release summary

Nick Bryan · Global Head of Real Estate · abrdn

Original IBR source ↗

abrdn appointed Nick Bryan as Global Head of Real Estate, succeeding Guy McKenna. Bryan had been with the firm for more than two decades and had held senior roles across its real estate investment business.

The appointment brought continuity to the firm's real estate platform while it continued to manage investments across sectors and markets. The original IBR article reported the change as part of the firm's leadership structure for real estate.

Company source: abrdn. This page preserves the historical appointment announcement; later role changes are outside the scope of the original item.

News: First schools install Great British Energy solar panels

7 June 2025 · Alfred Anate Mayaki

Original IBR source ↗

The first 11 schools in England's Great British Energy rooftop solar rollout were announced in June 2025, with estimated combined savings of £175,000 per year after installation. Further schools were expected to be announced over the summer, with participating schools planned to have panels installed by year end.

The rollout followed the government's announcement of £180 million to install rooftop solar at schools and hospitals. Around £80 million was allocated to approximately 200 schools in England, with a further £100 million for nearly 200 NHS sites, covering around one-third of NHS trusts.

The programme aims to reduce public-service energy bills and emissions. The government estimated that the projects could return savings to frontline services and, over around 30 years, deliver lifetime savings of up to £400 million for schools and the NHS.

At the time of the announcement, the government said only around one in five schools had solar panels installed. A typical school could save up to £25,000 annually with solar panels and complementary technologies such as batteries, depending on the installation and energy use.

Official source: UK Government. These are the estimates reported at the time of publication, not a statement of current rollout completion.

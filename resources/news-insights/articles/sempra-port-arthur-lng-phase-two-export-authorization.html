News: Port Arthur LNG Phase 2 receives non-FTA export authorization

30 May 2025 · Alfred Anate Mayaki

Original IBR source ↗

Sempra Infrastructure announced that the US Department of Energy had granted non-Free Trade Agreement (non-FTA) export authorization for Port Arthur LNG Phase 2. The authorization allows the project to export liquefied natural gas to countries that do not have a free-trade agreement with the United States.

The IBR article described the decision as a milestone in expanding US LNG export capacity and advancing the Port Arthur project. The project is expected to attract investment and support employment in the region, while contributing to debates about energy security, global gas demand and the transition to lower-carbon energy systems.

The authorization is a regulatory milestone and should not be interpreted on its own as confirmation that the project is fully constructed or operating.

Official source: Sempra Infrastructure.

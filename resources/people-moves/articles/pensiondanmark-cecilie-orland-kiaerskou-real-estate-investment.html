People Moves: PensionDanmark appoints Cecilie Orland Kiærskou as Real Estate Investment Director

27 November 2025 · By Alfred Anate Mayaki

Cecilie Orland Kiærskou · Head of Investments, Real Estate · PensionDanmark

Original IBR source / press release ↗

Copenhagen, Denmark (Press Release) -- PensionDanmark announced that its real estate department is consolidating investment activities into a new unit and increasing its focus on joint-venture partnerships and development projects. Cecilie Orland Kiærskou has been appointed Head of Investments to lead the strengthened investment function.

Kiærskou joins from Urban Partners, where she focused on responsible investments and major development projects, including retail development in Copenhagen's Århusgadekvarteret district. In her new role she will help ensure that PensionDanmark's real estate investments deliver stable, competitive returns for members while creating value in each project.

At the end of 2024, PensionDanmark's real estate portfolio was reported at DKK 32.4 billion (approximately €4.34 billion), spanning commercial property and new residential districts. The source said Kiærskou would assume the role on 1 February 2026.

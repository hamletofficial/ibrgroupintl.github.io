Welcome to the Members Area

Hello, Member — welcome to your secure members area.

Your account is authenticated through Firebase, so only a signed-in member can access this area and the member services linked from it. The members area is secure and safe to use for managing your account and communications briefs.

🔒
Security without the extra hurdle

Two-factor authentication (2FA) is an optional additional layer of protection. You do not need to upgrade to 2FA to use the Members Area, send a brief, or manage your submissions. If you want to review or change your security and account preferences, you can use Account Settings.

1

Send a brief to Communications

Use Media Relations / Requests to send a concise brief to our communications team.

  1. Open requests from the button below or the Members navigation.
  2. Enter the organisation and press-officer contact details.
  3. Add the headline, announcement type, press-release link, summary, and any useful notes such as audience, spokesperson availability or holding lines.
  4. Review the information and select Send Brief. Your submission is saved for the communications desk to review and follow up.
Send a Communications Brief
2

Manage your submissions

Use the Brief Archive to manage briefs submitted from your authenticated account.

  1. Open archive to view your submitted briefs.
  2. Use Search archive to find a particular submission.
  3. Select one or more briefs if you need to delete them, then choose Delete Selected.
  4. Use Refresh to reload your archive, Create New to start another brief, or Print when you need a record.
Manage My Submissions
3

Keep your member account up to date

Complete your profile if you want to receive communications from Premium, explore the Insights Archive, and use Account Settings to manage your preferences.

4

Discover the Members Directory

Our Members Directory is a convenient way to discover and connect with other members of the IBR community. It provides a useful member-facing view of participating professionals, helping you identify people and organisations you may wish to engage with while making your membership more connected and valuable.

Take a moment to explore the directory and see who is part of the wider community. You may find a useful professional connection, a relevant contact or an opportunity to broaden your network.

People Moves: Equilis France names new CEO

10 April 2026 · By Alfred Anate Mayaki

Constance Poublet · Chief Executive Officer · Equilis France

FRANCE (Press Release) -- Equilis is pleased to announce the appointment of Constance Poublet as Chief Executive Officer. With extensive experience in real estate development, strategic leadership, and international growth, she will guide Equilis into its next phase of innovation and sustainable expansion. Constance brings a strong track record of building high-performing teams, nurturing long-term partnerships, and delivering value for stakeholders.

Under her leadership, Equilis will continue to focus on responsible urban development, customer-centric solutions, and long-term resilience across all its markets.

She joins Equilis with the ambition to continue structuring and developing the French platform, in line with the group's strategy to strengthen its position in mixed-use developments and urban transformation across Europe.

"I am very pleased to be joining Equilis at a moment when the group is both well established and continuing to grow. What convinced me is the company's ability to combine entrepreneurial agility with a strong understanding of local contexts and real needs. In France, my priority will be to build on the momentum already in place. I look forward to working with the teams to pursue this trajectory together."

Constance Poublet, CEO Equilis France

"Constance brings a solid and well-rounded experience of our industry, with a strong understanding of both operational challenges and urban contexts. Her path reflects a consistent commitment to projects, teams and territories. I am confident she will continue to develop Equilis France with the same level of engagement and clarity."

Carl Mestdagh, Executive Chairman Equilis Europe

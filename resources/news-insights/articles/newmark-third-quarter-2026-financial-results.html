News: Newmark Group's Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

5 October 2026 · Newmark Group, Inc.

Original IBR source ↗

Newmark Group, Inc. (Nasdaq: NMRK) announced the timing for its third-quarter 2026 financial results and associated investor conference call.

The company said it planned to issue an advisory press release at 8:00 a.m. Eastern Time on Thursday, 29 October 2026, notifying the public that the full-text results release would be available through its investor relations and media channels.

Newmark is a commercial real estate advisory and services business serving institutional investors, corporations, property owners and occupiers. The announcement was a scheduling notice; it did not itself provide the quarter's financial results.

Official investor information: Newmark Investor Relations and Newmark Media.

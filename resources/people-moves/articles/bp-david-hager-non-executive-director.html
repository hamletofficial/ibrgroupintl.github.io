People Moves: BP appoints Devon Energy's David Hager as a Non-Executive Director

2 June 2025 · IBR media release summary

David Hager · Non-Executive Director · BP plc

Original IBR source / press release ↗

BP announced that David Hager had been appointed to its Board of Directors as a Non-Executive Director, commencing 2 June 2025. Hager brought more than four decades of experience in the energy industry, including serving as Executive Chair of Devon Energy from 2021 to 2023 and as its Chief Executive Officer from 2015 to 2021.

His earlier career included senior exploration and production roles at Devon Energy and Kerr-McGee. He was also a Non-Executive Director at MRC Global, an infrastructure products and services company.

The same announcement noted other board-committee changes, including Ian Tyler joining BP's Audit Committee and Satish Pai being nominated to succeed Melody Meyer as Chair of the Safety & Sustainability Committee following her planned retirement at the 2026 AGM.

Official source: BP. The IBR article also cited an EuropaWire press release.

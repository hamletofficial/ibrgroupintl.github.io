People Moves: Herbert Smith Freehills Kramer promotes 25 to partnership

24 April 2026 · By Alfred Anate Mayaki

Herbert Smith Freehills Kramer

Original IBR source / press release ↗

Herbert Smith Freehills Kramer announced the promotion of 25 new partners in its first global partner promotion round since the firm's launch in June 2025. The promotions, effective 1 May 2026, span multiple regions and practice areas, including energy, infrastructure, real estate, planning, private capital, disputes, transactions and technology.

The firm's announcement said the cohort strengthens its global presence and capabilities across key business corridors. Global CEO Justin D'Agostino and Chair and Senior Partner Rebecca Maslen-Stannage congratulated the new partners and described the promotions as an investment in the firm's future leadership and service offering.

The original IBR item lists the 25 appointees and their principal practice areas. Please consult the original IBR publication for the full list and the quoted statements.

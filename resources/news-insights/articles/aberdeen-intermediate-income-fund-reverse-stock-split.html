News: Aberdeen Intermediate Income Fund announces 1-for-6 reverse stock split

28 September 2026 · IBR article summary

Aberdeen Intermediate Income Fund (NYSE: MIN)

The Board of Trustees of Aberdeen Intermediate Income Fund (NYSE: MIN) approved a 1-for-6 reverse stock split of the fund's issued and outstanding common shares.

Under the announced terms, every six common shares would be consolidated into one common share. The transaction was to apply equally to shareholders and not affect their proportionate ownership interests, except for minor adjustments related to fractional shares.

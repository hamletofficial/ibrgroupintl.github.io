People Moves: BP appoints Murray Auchincloss as Chief Executive Officer

29 September 2023 · IBR media release summary

Murray Auchincloss · Chief Executive Officer · BP plc

Original IBR source ↗

BP announced Murray Auchincloss as Chief Executive Officer following the departure of Bernard Looney. Auchincloss had served as interim CEO and previously held the role of Chief Financial Officer.

The announcement represented a leadership transition at the energy company during a period of significant strategic focus on oil and gas operations, capital discipline and the energy transition.

Because executive roles may change over time, this page records the historical announcement rather than asserting that the appointment remains current.

Company source: BP.

Press Release: L&G proposes partnership model to unlock additional investment in affordable housing

28 April 2026 · Legal & General

Original IBR source ↗

Legal & General (L&G) published a white paper proposing a Partnership Registered Provider (Partnership RP) model intended to attract long-term institutional capital into affordable housing in England.

Under the model, a housing association and institutional investor form a joint venture. Housing associations transfer suitable homes to the partnership and use proceeds to build additional affordable homes and improve existing stock, while homes remain within the regulated social housing sector.

L&G's estimates suggest that, if adopted at scale, the model could unlock approximately £9bn in net new investment each year and support delivery of more than 80,000 affordable homes annually. These are projections based on assumptions set out in the white paper, not guaranteed outcomes.

The source also references L&G's existing partnership with Hyde Group and its historic investment in affordable and social housing.

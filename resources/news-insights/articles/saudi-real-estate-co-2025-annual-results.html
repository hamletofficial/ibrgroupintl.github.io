News: Saudi Real Estate Co announces annual financial results for 2025

27 April 2026 · Alfred Anate Mayaki

Original IBR source ↗

Saudi Real Estate Co (Al Akaria) announced audited annual financial results for the year ended 31 December 2025. The company said the year involved work to strengthen recurring revenue, optimise its asset base and improve operational efficiency, while contributing to Saudi Vision 2030 objectives.

Revenue decreased by 5.4%, equivalent to SAR 108 million, compared with the previous year. Infrastructure-project revenue declined by SAR 193 million, which the company attributed to lower activity in construction and development contracts, delays in execution and awards, and competitive tender pricing. Construction-segment revenue fell by approximately 91%.

Projects and contracts

The company highlighted a SAR 425 million contract for the Sidra 2 residential complex in Riyadh, covering 310 villas, and a SAR 338 million contract for Al Narjis Business Park, a mixed-use development on a 40,000-square-metre site in Riyadh.

Al Akaria said it continued to focus on project management, resource allocation, selective bidding and diversification to mitigate market fluctuations. The full audited financial statements and notes were made available through the company's reporting channels and the Saudi Exchange.

Official source: Saudi Real Estate Co (Al Akaria). Figures above are summarised from the original IBR publication; consult the company's audited report for full details.

News: Barings completes sale of four UK logistics assets

21 May 2025 · Alfred Anate Mayaki

Original IBR source ↗

Barings completed the off-market sale of four UK logistics assets located in Bristol (Avonmouth), Daventry, Warrington and Nottingham. The properties were sold to joint-venture partners Copley Point Capital and Sixth Street on behalf of a core European real estate strategy.

The portfolio comprised fully let assets in established distribution locations with long leases. The Bristol holding included two units totalling approximately 329,000 square feet; Daventry comprised a 238,543-square-foot warehouse; Warrington totalled 128,543 square feet; and the Nottingham property comprised a 113,168-square-foot warehouse on Sherwood Business Park.

Barings said the sale followed asset-management work that had increased income by 20 per cent during the holding period. It planned to redeploy capital into logistics, living sectors and selected office assets across its core European markets.

Barings was advised by ACRE Capital Partners, while Cushman & Wakefield advised the buyers.

Official source: Barings.

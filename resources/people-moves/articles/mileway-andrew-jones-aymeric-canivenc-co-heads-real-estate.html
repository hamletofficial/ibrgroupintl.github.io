People Moves: Mileway promotes Andrew Jones and Aymeric Canivenc to Co-Heads of Real Estate

17 December 2025 · By Alfred Anate Mayaki

Andrew Jones and Aymeric Canivenc · Co-Heads of Real Estate · Mileway

Original IBR source / press release ↗

Amsterdam, Netherlands (Press Release) -- Mileway, the pan-European last-mile logistics real estate company, announced the appointment of Andrew Jones and Aymeric Canivenc as Co-Heads of Real Estate, effective 1 January 2026. Their appointments follow Chief Real Estate Officer Thomas ten Bokum's decision to step down at the end of the year to take an extended break and spend more time with his family.

Jones and Canivenc have each played a role in strengthening Mileway's presence in two of its strategic markets and shaping its collaborative culture.

"We are delighted to appoint Andrew and Aymeric as Co-Heads of Real Estate. Both have been instrumental in strengthening our presence in two of our most strategic markets and in shaping a strong, collaborative culture across the business. Their promotions reflect our commitment to growing talent from within, and we look forward to seeing them lead Mileway's Real Estate function."

David McClure, CEO of Mileway

"Since Mileway was founded in 2019, Thomas has played a pivotal role in shaping the company and his leadership has been instrumental in positioning the company for long-term success. We are deeply grateful for his many contributions and wish him every success in his future endeavours."

David McClure, CEO of Mileway

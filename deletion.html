Account and Data Deletion

Effective date: October 2026

Your choice

You can delete your Impact in Business Recruitment account yourself. The option is on the Account Settings page. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. After it completes, that account can no longer be used to sign in.

How to delete your account

Follow these steps in the app:

  1. Open the site and sign in at Sign in. You must be signed in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Account Settings. From a signed-in page, use the members menu and choose Account Settings (the gear item). You can also go directly to /settings.html.
  3. On Account Settings, review the account details (name, email, user ID and last login) so you are sure this is the right account.
  4. Select the red Delete Your Account button under the account details card.
  5. Confirm in the dialog that says: “Are you sure you want to permanently delete your account? This cannot be undone.” Choose OK to continue, or Cancel to keep the account.
  6. If the app asks you to sign in again, complete the Google sign-in prompt. This is required when the current session is not recent enough to delete the account. After you reauthenticate, deletion continues automatically.
  7. When deletion succeeds, the app shows “Your account has been deleted.” and returns you to the sign-in page.

If you are not signed in, the button is not available. Sign in first, then return to Account Settings. If deletion fails, the app shows an error message. Sign in again and repeat the steps, or contact us using the address below.

What this deletes

The Delete Your Account button deletes the signed-in authentication account, including the sign-in identity tied to that user ID. You will be signed out and will not be able to access members-only areas with that account.

Profile and other records stored separately from the authentication account (for example a public profile document linked to your user ID) are not always removed by the button alone. To ask us to delete remaining personal data associated with the account, email support@mail.hamlet-ai.co.uk from the address on the account, include your user ID if you still have it, and state that you want associated data deleted.

What we may keep

We may retain limited information where we have to, including payment and invoice records, records needed to resolve a dispute, and information we must keep to meet a legal, tax or accounting obligation. Retained records are kept only for as long as that purpose requires and are not used to restore a deleted account. See our Privacy Policy for how we retain personal data.

If you cannot use the button

If you cannot sign in, or the delete button does not complete, email support@mail.hamlet-ai.co.uk with the subject “Account deletion request”. Include the email address on the account and, if you know it, the user ID shown on Account Settings. We will verify the request and delete the account and associated personal data we hold, subject to the retention limits above.

Changes

We may update this page if the deletion steps change. We will post changes here with an updated effective date.

Contact

For questions about account or data deletion, contact us at support@mail.hamlet-ai.co.uk.

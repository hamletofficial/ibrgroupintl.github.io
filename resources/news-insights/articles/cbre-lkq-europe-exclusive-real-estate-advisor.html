News: CBRE appointed exclusive European real estate advisor for LKQ Europe

4 November 2025 · CBRE

Original IBR source ↗

CBRE has announced its appointment as the exclusive European real estate advisor for LKQ Europe (LKQ) on a five-year contract, effective from 1 January 2026. The partnership is intended to support LKQ's real estate strategy and its operational footprint across Europe.

LKQ Europe is a distributor of automotive aftermarket parts for cars, commercial vans and industrial vehicles. It employs approximately 26,000 people and operates a network of more than 900 branches, supplying parts to over 100,000 independent workshops across more than 18 European countries.

Through its collaboration with CBRE, LKQ aims to streamline its real estate strategy and ensure its property portfolio supports the needs of its extensive European network.

The original IBR publication linked to CBRE's own press release. This summary preserves the announcement's key facts and attribution.

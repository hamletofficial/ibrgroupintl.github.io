News: DBS anchors SGD 692 million green loan for Hong Leong Holdings' Tengah development

3 June 2025 · Alfred Anate Mayaki

Original IBR source ↗

A consortium led by Hong Leong Holdings, with joint-venture partners GuocoLand and CSC Land Group, secured an SGD 692 million green loan to finance a nature-aligned residential development in Tengah, Singapore. DBS acted as anchor lender and sole green-loan adviser, contributing SGD 484 million, with OCBC providing the remainder.

The financing was structured in accordance with recognised Green Loan Principles. It is intended to support green infrastructure and ecological integration in a district planned as a car-lite, nature-oriented town. Tengah spans about 700 hectares and is expected to provide approximately 42,000 homes when fully developed.

The private mixed-use development in Tengah Garden Avenue was planned to comprise around 860 homes with integrated retail amenities. Its stated design features include layered landscaping, green spaces, biodiversity corridors, native planting and digital construction coordination to reduce waste and improve efficiency.

The project illustrates how sustainable finance can be used to support urban development that combines residential supply, retail services and ecological considerations.

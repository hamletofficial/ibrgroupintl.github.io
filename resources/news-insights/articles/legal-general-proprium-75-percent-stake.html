News: L&G targets global markets with 75% acquisition of Proprium Capital Partners

20 May 2025 · Alfred Anate Mayaki

Original IBR source ↗

Legal & General announced an agreement to acquire a 75% stake in Proprium Capital Partners, a global real estate investor. The proposed transaction was intended to combine complementary capabilities and accelerate L&G's private-markets expansion into additional geographies.

Proprium, founded in 2013, invests in real estate operating companies across sectors including residential, student accommodation, hospitality and logistics. Its investments included a&o hostels in Germany, Admiral Taverns in the UK and Avid in Australia.

L&G set a target of growing its private-markets platform to £85 billion in assets under management by 2028, spanning real estate, infrastructure, private credit and venture capital. Proprium's management team was expected to continue operating independently in day-to-day matters within the wider L&G platform.

The announcement positioned the deal as a way to broaden L&G's international reach and expand investment strategies for institutional clients.

Official source: Legal & General.
